# Parsing Queries

A personal blog exploring the intersection of technology, design, and human behavior.

**Live site:** [issamohamed.com](https://issamohamed.com)

## About

Parsing Queries is where I write long-form essays examining the often-overlooked tensions in how we interact with technology. From the ethics of content moderation to the psychology of online privacy, I'm interested in the messy, complicated spaces where technical systems meet human intuition.
